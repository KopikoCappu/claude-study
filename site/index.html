Your notes, marked up by AI.

Claude Study is a handwriting-first notes app with a study buddy built in. Lasso your work and it circles the mistake, checks what's right, and explains it in the margin, right on your page.

Write like paper. Get help like a tutor.

Everything you expect from a notes app, plus an assistant that can see your page and write on it.

Find my mistake

Lasso a problem and the step that went wrong gets circled, with a note explaining why.

Explain

Ask about any equation, diagram or paragraph and get a plain-language explanation, with math rendered properly.

Handwriting to text

Turn messy handwriting into clean typed text and equations you can drop back onto the page.

A pen that feels right

Pressure-sensitive pen, highlighter, shapes, eraser and lasso, plus pen-only mode so your palm doesn't draw.

Pages, folders, PDFs

Paged notebooks, colorful folders, photos of worksheets, PDF import and PDF export.

Sync everywhere

An optional account keeps your notes in sync between your tablet, phone and laptop.

How it works

Three steps, no copy-pasting screenshots into a chatbot.

Write

Take notes or work a problem by hand, just like on paper.

Lasso

Circle the part you're stuck on and tap Find mistake, Explain, or ask your own question.

Read the margins

The answer shows up in a side panel, and the AI marks up your page in red and green ink.

Your notes stay yours.

Claude Study is built so we never see your work and never spend your money behind your back.

  • Bring your own AI key. Use your Claude API key and pay Anthropic directly. It's stored only on your device, encrypted, and never uploaded.
  • No ads, no tracking. No analytics SDKs, and we never sell your data.
  • Local first. Notes live on your device. An account and cloud sync are optional.
  • Delete anytime. Remove your account and everything in it from the app in two taps. Read the privacy policy.
