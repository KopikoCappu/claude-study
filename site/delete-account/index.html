In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Claude Study and tap your email address at the bottom of the home-screen sidebar.
  2. Tap Delete account, then Delete forever.

By email

Send an email from your account's email address to podplananything@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Claude Study account". We'll delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

Your account (email address and password) and every note, folder, photo and chat stored in it are permanently deleted. Nothing is kept, apart from short-lived hosting logs, which expire on their own within 30 days.

Your AI key is never stored by us. It stays on your device until you remove it under AI key in the sidebar or uninstall the app.
